COMMON QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Q: Can I use pre-ground coffee?
A: Yes, the bypass doser allows you to use pre-ground coffee for a single serving.

Q: How many cups can I make before refilling?
A: The 2-liter water tank provides approximately 10-15 cups depending on beverage size.

Q: Is the grinder adjustable?
A: Yes, the ceramic burr grinder has 13 fineness settings from coarse to very fine.

Q: Does it work with alternative milk (soy, almond, oat)?
A: Yes, the automatic milk frother works excellently with all types of milk, including plant-based alternatives.

Q: How often do I need to descale?
A: This depends on water hardness, but typically every 2-3 months. The machine will alert you when descaling is needed.

Q: Can I adjust the coffee temperature?
A: Yes, you can choose from 3 temperature settings: Standard (85°C), High (90°C), and Maximum (95°C).

Q: What's the difference between the milk frother settings?
A: You can adjust foam density from light (for latte) to dense (for cappuccino) in 5 levels.

Q: Is it easy to clean?
A: Yes, the brew unit is removable and dishwasher safe. The milk system has an automatic rinse function.
