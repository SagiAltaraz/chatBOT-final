EvoPhone X - Premium Smartphone

OVERVIEW
The EvoPhone X represents the pinnacle of mobile technology, combining cutting-edge hardware with an intuitive user experience. Released in 2024, this flagship device sets new standards for smartphone performance and design.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Display: 6.7-inch AMOLED screen with 120Hz refresh rate and 2800x1290 resolution
Processor: OctoCore X9 chipset (3.2 GHz max clock speed)
RAM: 12GB LPDDR5
Storage Options: 256GB, 512GB, 1TB (non-expandable)
Camera System:
  - Main: 108MP wide-angle lens with OIS
  - Ultra-wide: 48MP, 120-degree field of view
  - Telephoto: 12MP with 5x optical zoom
  - Front: 32MP selfie camera
Battery: 5000mAh with 65W fast charging, 30W wireless charging
Operating System: EvoOS 5.0 (based on Android 14)
Connectivity: 5G, WiFi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC, USB-C
Dimensions: 162.5 x 75.8 x 8.9 mm
Weight: 198 grams
Water Resistance: IP68 rating

COMMON QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Q: Does the EvoPhone X support wireless charging?
A: Yes, it supports 30W wireless charging and 5W reverse wireless charging for accessories.

Q: Is the storage expandable?
A: No, the EvoPhone X does not have a microSD card slot. Choose your storage capacity carefully at purchase.

Q: What colors are available?
A: The EvoPhone X comes in Midnight Black, Arctic Silver, Ocean Blue, and Sunset Gold.

Q: Is there a headphone jack?
A: No, the EvoPhone X uses USB-C for audio or wireless Bluetooth connection.

Q: What's included in the box?
A: The package includes the phone, a USB-C cable, a SIM ejector tool, and documentation. The charger is sold separately.

Q: How long is the warranty?
A: Standard warranty is 1 year, with optional extended warranty available for purchase.

Q: Does it support 5G networks?
A: Yes, the EvoPhone X supports both Sub-6 and mmWave 5G bands.
