EcoVolt Z - Premium Electric Vehicle

OVERVIEW
The EcoVolt Z is a luxury all-electric sedan that combines sustainable technology with exceptional performance. Designed for the modern driver who values both environmental responsibility and driving dynamics, the EcoVolt Z delivers an uncompromising electric vehicle experience.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Powertrain: Dual-motor all-wheel drive
Total Power Output: 670 horsepower (500 kW)
Torque: 1,050 Nm
Battery: 100 kWh lithium-ion battery pack
Range: 520 km (EPA estimated) on a single charge
Acceleration: 0-100 km/h in 3.2 seconds
Top Speed: 250 km/h (electronically limited)
Drive Modes: Eco, Comfort, Sport, and Custom
Regenerative Braking: 3 levels of adjustable regeneration

COMMON QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Q: How much does it cost to charge the EcoVolt Z?
A: At average electricity rates ($0.13 per kWh), a full charge costs approximately $13 USD, providing 520 km of range.

Q: Does cold weather affect the range?
A: Yes, in temperatures below -10°C, you can expect approximately 20-30% range reduction. The battery has thermal management to minimize this effect.

Q: Can I tow with the EcoVolt Z?
A: No, the EcoVolt Z is not rated for towing. It's designed as a performance sedan rather than a utility vehicle.

Q: What's the estimated annual maintenance cost?
A: Much lower than gasoline vehicles. Expect approximately $200-400 per year for tire rotation, brake fluid, and cabin filter replacement.

Q: How long will the battery last?
A: The battery is designed to retain at least 90% capacity after 8 years. With proper care, it should last 15-20 years.

Q: Can I charge at any public charging station?
A: The EcoVolt Z uses the standard CCS connector, compatible with most public charging networks in North America and Europe.

Q: Does it support vehicle-to-grid (V2G)?
A: Currently, no. V2G capability is planned for future model years pending regulatory approval.
