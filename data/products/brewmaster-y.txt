BrewMaster Y - Professional Coffee Maker

OVERVIEW
The BrewMaster Y is a premium automatic espresso and coffee machine designed for coffee enthusiasts who demand café-quality beverages at home.

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
Water Tank: 2-liter capacity (removable)
Bean Hopper: 300g capacity with airtight seal
Pump Pressure: 19 bar Italian pump
Grinder: Ceramic burr grinder with 13 grinding settings
Boiler System: Dual thermoblock technology
Power: 1450W, 220-240V
Heating Time: Ready in 40 seconds
Dimensions: 38 x 29 x 43 cm
Weight: 9.8 kg
Display: 4.3-inch color touchscreen
Milk System: Automatic milk frother with adjustable foam density

- Americano
- Cappuccino
- Latte
- Flat White
- Macchiato
- Hot Water (for tea)
- Customizable recipes with saved profiles

FEATURES

- Pre-Infusion System: Ensures optimal flavor extraction
- Automatic Cleaning: Self-cleaning milk system and descaling alerts
- Energy Saving Mode: Auto shut-off after 30 minutes of inactivity
- Cup Warmer: Top plate keeps cups warm
