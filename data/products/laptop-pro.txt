COMMON QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Q: Can I upgrade the RAM and storage?
A: Yes, the TechBook Pro has 2x SO-DIMM slots (supports up to 64GB total) and one M.2 NVMe slot. RAM and storage are user-replaceable.

Q: Does it support external monitors?
A: Yes, it supports up to 3 external displays: 2x via Thunderbolt 4 (up to 4K at 120Hz each) and 1x via HDMI 2.1 (4K at 120Hz).

Q: How is the keyboard for typing?
A: The keyboard has excellent 1.5mm travel distance with tactile feedback. The layout includes a numeric keypad, and backlighting has 3 brightness levels.

Q: Can it run demanding games?
A: Yes, the RTX 4070 GPU handles most modern games at high settings. The 165Hz display is excellent for competitive gaming.

Q: Is it suitable for video editing?
A: Absolutely. The powerful i9 processor, 32GB RAM, and RTX 4070 GPU make it excellent for 4K video editing in Adobe Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut Pro (via Boot Camp).

Q: How loud are the fans under load?
A: In Performance mode during heavy tasks, fans reach approximately 45-48 dB. Silent mode keeps noise under 35 dB but reduces performance.

Q: Does it get hot during use?
A: The chassis stays cool during normal use. Under heavy load, the keyboard area may feel warm (35-40°C) but not uncomfortable. The vapor chamber cooling system is very effective.

Q: Can I charge it with a phone charger?
A: USB-C PD chargers (65W+) will charge the laptop, but slowly. For full performance, use the included 180W charger.

Q: What's the warranty?
A: Standard 2-year international warranty. Extended warranty and accidental damage protection available for purchase.
